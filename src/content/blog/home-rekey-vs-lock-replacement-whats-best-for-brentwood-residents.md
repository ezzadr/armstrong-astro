When moving into a new home or experiencing a security concern, one of the first questions Middle Tennessee homeowners ask is: **"Should I rekey my existing locks or replace the entire hardware?"**

Many homeowners mistakenly assume that changing their keys requires purchasing brand new locksets and doorknobs for every exterior door. In reality, **rekeying your locks can save you 60% to 75%** while delivering 100% brand new key security.

Here is a practical guide from master locksmith Rahim Ezzadpanah on how lock rekeying works, when you should rekey, and when total replacement makes sense.

## 🔑 What Is Lock Rekeying? (How It Works)

**Rekeying** is the professional process of altering the internal mechanism of a lock cylinder so that old keys will no longer operate it, and a brand new set of keys will work instead.

* We remove the lock cylinder from your door handle or deadbolt.
* We remove the internal brass bottom pins and master pins.
* We calculate a new pinning combination and install fresh, precision-matched bottom pins.
* We cut brand new factory keys that match the new pin heights.

The exterior door handles, decorative deadbolts, and hardware finish remain completely untouched. The entire process takes approximately **10 to 15 minutes per door** when performed by a licensed locksmith.

## 🏠 When You Should Rekey Your Locks

Rekeying is the ideal, cost-effective security solution in the following common situations:

### 1. You Just Bought a New Home
When purchasing a new house in Nashville or Williamson County, previous owners, real estate agents, home inspectors, contractors, and cleaners may still hold copies of the keys. Rekeying gives you total peace of mind that **only your family** possesses access.

### 2. Tenant Turnover on Rental Properties
Landlords and property managers can rekey lock cylinders between tenants rather than buying new deadbolts each year.

### 3. Simplifying to One "Master Key" (Single Key System)
Tired of carrying separate keys for the front door, back door, garage entry, and patio deadbolt? If your locks share the same brand (e.g., all Schlage or all Kwikset), a locksmith can rekey every lock in your home to **operate with a single master key**.

### 4. Lost or Stolen Keys
If a family member or babysitter lost a key, rekeying the cylinder immediately renders the missing key completely useless.

## 🛠️ When Should You Fully Replace Your Locks?

While rekeying is usually the best choice, complete lock replacement is recommended in these specific scenarios:

* **Damaged, Worn, or Corroded Hardware:** If the latch is sticking, the bolt is grinding, or the finish is heavily weathered.
* **Upgrading to Smart Keyless Electronic Deadbolts:** If you want keypad entry, smartphone app unlocking (Schlage Encode, Yale Assure, or August), or temporary guest PIN codes.
* **Upgrading Security Grades:** If your home currently has basic builder-grade Grade 3 locks, upgrading to **ANSI Grade 1 or Grade 2 commercial-grade deadbolts** significantly improves kick-in resistance.
* **Mismatched Keyways:** If your front door is Schlage and your back door is Kwikset, they cannot be keyed alike without changing one of the brands.
