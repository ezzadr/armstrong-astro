## One Visit to the Shop Instead of Two Dealership Trips

A customer came to [our shop at 208 Thompson Lane](/nashville-locksmith-storefront/) today with her husband. They came down from **Brentwood, Tennessee**, and she wanted a spare key for two of their vehicles: a **[Toyota C-HR](/toyota-car-key-replacement/)** and an **[Audi](/audi-car-keys/)**.

Normally with two different car brands, you would have to go to two separate dealerships, book two appointments, and pay dealer prices twice.

## Both Spare Keys Made While They Waited

Since we keep both Japanese and European smart keys in stock at the shop, I made a spare key for both vehicles right there in one visit:

- **Toyota C-HR Smart Key:** Programmed the push-to-start remote to the car and cut the emergency blade on our high-security laser key machine.
- **Audi Smart Key:** Programmed the 4-button Audi smart fob and cut its emergency door blade at the counter.

We saved her and her husband quite a good amount of money compared to the dealership price, and they knocked out both cars in a single trip.
