## Installing the New Adams Rite Mortise Deadlatch

Commercial glass doors with narrow aluminum frames take a specific mortise lock body that fits inside the frame tube. On this door, Kawa took out the old worn unit and installed a new **Adams Rite (ASSA ABLOY) mortise deadlatch** paired with the **interior push exit paddle**:

- **Commercial Deadlatch Security:** If you look closely at the silver faceplate on the edge of the door, you can see the main latch bolt and the smaller deadlatch plunger right underneath it. When the door shuts, that small plunger stays pressed in so nobody can slip a card or tool into the frame gap to push the latch back.
- **Easy Push Paddle Inside:** From the inside of the shop, the owner and his employees don't need to fumble with a key or a tiny thumbturn—they just push the wide silver paddle and walk right out.

## Re-Keyed So All His Business Locks Work on the Same Key

Whenever you pull a brand-new commercial lock cylinder out of the box, it comes with its own factory key that won't match anything else on the building. Nobody running a business wants one key for the front glass door and another key for the back door or office.

We **re-keyed the locks so the customer would have all his locks on the same key**, and cut him a fresh set of keys right there at the door. For small businesses, **we always recommend the Schlage brand**—Schlage cylinders hold up much better to heavy daily commercial use than cheap off-brand cylinders, the keys don't wear down or snap easily, and it makes matching your front storefront door to the rest of your building simple and reliable.
