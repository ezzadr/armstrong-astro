    What Car Key Replacement Really Involves

    Replacing a modern car key is not the same as cutting a spare at a hardware store. Almost every vehicle built in the last 20 years pairs a cut blade with a transponder chip or a proximity fob that has to be programmed to your car's immobilizer before the engine will start. At our Thompson Ln shop we handle both halves of that job in house: we cut the blade to your lock, then program the chip through the OBD port or, when a vehicle needs it, by reading the immobilizer data directly.

    If you have lost every key and there is no working original, we can still generate a new key from the vehicle itself. That all key lost situation takes longer and costs more than adding a spare, so it helps to tell us which situation you are in when you call.

    Keys and Fobs We Replace

    We cut and program keys for almost every make on the road, whether it is domestic, Asian, or European. The common types we handle are:

    • Transponder keys the chipped metal keys used on most cars from the late 1990s onward.
    • Remote head keys a cut key with the lock and unlock buttons built into the head.
    • Smart keys and proximity fobs the push to start systems where the fob stays in your pocket.
    • Flip and switchblade keys common on Volkswagen, Audi, and many European models.
    • High security laser cut keys the thicker sidewinder blades used on newer vehicles.

    Several makes have their own page with model detail: Toyota, Honda, Kia, BMW, Mercedes, Audi, and other European car keys. If your make is not listed, call 615-625-8000 and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it same day.

    What a Replacement Key Costs

    Price depends on your vehicle. A basic transponder key for an older sedan is one of the cheapest jobs we do, while a proximity fob for a late model luxury vehicle costs more because the fob itself is expensive and the programming is more involved. The biggest single factor is whether you still have a working key: adding a spare is quicker and cheaper than an all key lost job where we originate a key from scratch.

    In almost every case we come in well under what a dealership charges, and we do not tow your car to do it. You can see typical ranges on our Nashville locksmith pricing page, or call for a firm quote on your exact year, make, and model.

    Car Key Replacement FAQ

    Can you make a car key with no original?
    Yes. If every key is lost we can originate a new one from the vehicle. It takes longer than a spare, so let us know when you book.

    How long does it take?
    Most spare keys are done in 20 to 45 minutes once we are with the car. All key lost jobs and some European models take longer.

    Do you come to me?
    Yes. We offer mobile service across Nashville and the surrounding areas, and we also have a walk in shop on Thompson Ln.

    Will a new key affect my existing keys?
    Adding a key does not disable your other working keys. In an all key lost job we reset the system, which does erase any keys that are not present.
