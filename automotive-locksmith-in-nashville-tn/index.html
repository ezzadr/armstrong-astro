2007 Porsche 911: All Keys Lost, Made In-House

A customer in Woodbine lost all the keys to their 2007 Porsche 911. The local Porsche dealership quoted them $1,300 and a frustrating 3-week wait just to order a replacement key from Germany.

For this generation of 911, there are no shortcuts: the KESSY immobilizer module must be extracted to originate the key data. We handled the entire job in-house at our Thompson Ln shop. We safely pulled the KESSY module, laser-cut a new key, and had the car running in just 2 hours—saving the customer time and money.
