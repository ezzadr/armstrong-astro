How It Works

Our 4-Step On-Site Key Replacement Process

1

ID & Ownership Check

We verify vehicle registration/title and government ID to ensure strict legal and security compliance.

2

Lock Decoding & Laser Cut

We decode your mechanical door lock cylinder with Lishi optics and CNC laser-cut the emergency metal blade.

3

Immobilizer Programming

We sync the transponder to CAS2, CAS3, CAS4+, FEM, or BDC modules directly using dealer diagnostic tablets.

4

Testing & Key Deletion

We verify push-button start, Comfort Access, and remote trunk release, then wipe any lost keys from memory.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About BMW Keys

Q:Can you make a BMW key if all keys are lost?

Yes! We decode the mechanical door lock cylinder with specialized Lishi tools and program brand-new smart proximity keys directly to your BMW's CAS, FEM, or BDC immobilizer module on-site. No towing is required.

Q:How much do I save compared to the BMW dealer?

Dealerships typically charge $650 to $1,200+ plus towing and make you wait over a week. Armstrong Locksmith provides same-day on-site service, typically saving customers $300 to $650.

Q:Will Comfort Access push-to-start work?

Yes. We supply and program OEM-quality high-security smart keys that fully support BMW Comfort Access, proximity engine push-button starting, remote trunk release, and panic functions.

Q:Can I bring my BMW to your Thompson Ln shop?

Yes! If you have at least one working key, you can drive to our storefront at 208 Thompson Ln in Nashville and get a duplicate key made while you wait with no mobile dispatch fee.
