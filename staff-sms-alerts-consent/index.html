Staff SMS Alerts Consent

Consent and enrollment information for internal website-form dispatch notifications sent to authorized Armstrong Locksmith staff members.

1. Purpose of SMS Alerts

Armstrong Locksmith Inc uses SMS only to send internal operational dispatch alerts to authorized staff members when a customer submits a service request through our website. These alerts enable our technicians to respond promptly to customer-requested emergency automotive, residential, and commercial locksmith services. They are strictly internal dispatch notifications and are not marketing, advertising, or promotional messages.

2. Who May Enroll & Consent Terms

Enrollment is limited exclusively to authorized Armstrong Locksmith employees and field technicians. Before receiving alerts, staff members provide express written or digital consent to receive operational dispatch notifications on their designated mobile device.

Consent is not a condition of purchasing any product or service. Staff members may opt in by sending START from their registered phone number to Armstrong Locksmith's verified notification system.

3. Message Frequency & Commands

  • Content: Messages contain customer lead and service-request details (job type, location, vehicle model).
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies dynamically based on customer website inquiries.
  • Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.
  • Opt-Out: Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from SMS notifications.
  • Help: Reply HELP or call (615) 625-8000 for technical assistance.

4. Privacy & Data Protection

Mobile phone numbers, opt-in records, and SMS consent information will never be sold, rented, leased, or shared with third parties, data brokers, or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information is shared only with verified telecommunications service providers (such as Twilio and carrier aggregators) as strictly necessary to deliver operational dispatch alerts or as required by law. For full details, review our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
