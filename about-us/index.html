Our Story

Meet Rahim & The Armstrong Locksmith Family

A real, local Nashville locksmith with over two decades of craftsmanship, dedicated to transparent pricing and dependable security.

Rahim Ezzadpanah

Founder & Master Locksmith
Our Machine Workshop at 208 Thompson Ln

Founded in Nashville in 2015 on Principles of Honesty & Craftsmanship

With over 20 years of hands-on experience in the locksmith industry, I founded Armstrong Locksmith in 2015 to give Nashville a reliable, honest security partner. After years of working for another local company, I realized that our community needed a real physical storefront they could visit on Thompson Lane—not an anonymous dispatch center.

While our dedicated technicians handle all commercial door hardware and residential lockouts, my personal specialty is high-end automotive security. We are fully equipped to cut and program keys for all domestic, foreign, and luxury European vehicles (including BMW, Audi, Mercedes, and Land Rover) right at our 208 Thompson Lane shop or via our mobile dispatch units.
