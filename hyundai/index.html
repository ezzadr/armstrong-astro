Frequently Asked Questions About Hyundai Keys

Q:Do you program Hyundai push-to-start smart fobs?

Yes! We cut and program proximity smart keys and flip remotes for all Hyundai models on-site with OBD2 diagnostic tools.

Q:What if all keys to my Hyundai are lost?

We decode the mechanical door lock cylinder with Lishi tools, laser-cut the emergency blade, and program a brand new smart key from scratch on location.

Q:How much do I save compared to the Hyundai dealer?

Dealerships typically charge $350 to $650+ plus towing and make you wait nearly a week. We offer same-day service saving you $150 to $350.

Q:Can I drive to your storefront on Thompson Ln?

Yes! If you have a working key, bring your Hyundai to 208 Thompson Ln in Nashville and get duplicate keys cut and programmed while you wait.
