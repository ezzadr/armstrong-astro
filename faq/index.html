Frequently Asked Questions
About Nashville Locksmith Services

Straightforward, transparent answers from our master locksmith team regarding automotive key programming, mobile response times, storefront walk-ins, and pricing.

Can you make a replacement car key if I lost all my keys?

Yes! In an "all-keys-lost" scenario, you do not need to tow your car to the dealer. Our mobile locksmith van comes directly to your location in Nashville or Middle Tennessee. We decode your door lock cylinder using precision Lishi tools, cut factory-spec laser key blades, and program the transponder chip or smart fob directly into your vehicle's immobilizer ECU on-site.

How much money do I save compared to the car dealership?

Most customers save between 30% and 50% compared to dealership quotes. Furthermore, you eliminate the $150+ towing fee and the typical 3-to-7 day dealership delay because we complete the job on-site in about 20 to 30 minutes.

Do you have a real physical shop I can visit?

Yes! We are not an out-of-state call center or a trunk-only operation. We own and operate a physical storefront at 208 Thompson Ln, Nashville, TN 37211. You are welcome to walk in for key duplication, spare fob programming, and lock rekeying during our storefront hours (Mon–Fri 8AM–6PM, Sat 10AM–4PM).

What is the difference between rekeying and replacing locks?

Rekeying changes only the internal tumbler pins inside your existing lock cylinder so old keys no longer work. Replacing involves removing and buying entirely new deadbolts and handle sets. Rekeying is 90% of the time the smarter, faster, and much cheaper choice ($20/cylinder in shop).

How fast can a locksmith arrive for emergency lockouts?

Our typical mobile response time is 15 to 25 minutes across Nashville, Brentwood, Franklin, Antioch, Green Hills, East Nashville, and Donelson.
