Meet Rahim Ezzadpanah

I opened Armstrong Locksmith in 2015 after working in the locksmith trade for over twenty years. My goal was simple: give Nashville drivers, homeowners, and business owners a real physical locksmith shop they can walk into—not an out-of-state call center.

While our mobile vans handle home lock re-keys, lockouts, and commercial storefront doors every day, my personal specialty is European and luxury car keys (BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Land Rover, Porsche, and Bentley). We cut and program smart keys on-site or at our counter so you don't have to tow your car to the dealership.
