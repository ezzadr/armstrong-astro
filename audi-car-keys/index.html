How It Works

Our 4-Step On-Site Key Replacement Process

1

ID & Ownership Check

We verify vehicle registration/title and government ID to ensure strict legal and security compliance.

2

Lock Decoding & Laser Cut

We decode the emergency door lock cylinder with Lishi HU66 optics and CNC laser-cut the metal blade.

3

Immobilizer Programming

We connect directly to BCM2, MQB, and MLB security modules to program factory-quality smart proximity keys.

4

Testing & Key Deletion

We test start, lock/unlock, and Comfort Access, then delete lost keys from vehicle memory.
