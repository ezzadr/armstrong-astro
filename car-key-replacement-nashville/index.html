What Car Key Replacement Really Involves

Replacing a modern car key is not the same as cutting a spare at a hardware store. Almost every vehicle built in the last 20 years pairs a cut blade with a transponder chip or a proximity fob that has to be programmed to your car’s immobilizer before the engine will start.

At our Thompson Ln shop, we handle both halves of that job in-house: we cut the blade to your lock using precision computer-controlled laser cutters, then program the chip through the OBD port or, when a vehicle needs it, by reading the immobilizer data directly.

If you have lost every key and there is no working original, we can still generate a new key from the vehicle itself. That all-keys-lost situation takes longer and costs more than adding a spare, so it helps to tell us which situation you are in when you call.

Keys and Fobs We Replace

We cut and program keys for almost every make on the road, whether it is domestic, Asian, or European. The common types we handle include:

  • Transponder Keys: The chipped metal keys used on most cars from the late 1990s onward.
  • Remote Head Keys: A cut key with lock and unlock buttons built directly into the key head.
  • Smart Keys & Proximity Fobs: Push-to-start systems where the fob stays in your pocket.
  • Flip & Switchblade Keys: Common on Volkswagen, Audi, Kia, and many European models.
  • High-Security Laser Cut Keys: The thicker internal track sidewinder blades used on newer vehicles.

What a Replacement Key Costs

Price depends on your vehicle. A basic transponder key for an older sedan is one of the most affordable jobs we do, while a proximity fob for a late-model luxury vehicle costs more because the fob itself is expensive and the programming is more involved.

The biggest single factor is whether you still have a working key: adding a duplicate spare is quicker and cheaper than an all-keys-lost job where we originate a key from scratch.

In almost every case we come in well under what a dealership charges, and we do not require you to tow your car to do it. You can see typical ranges on our Nashville locksmith pricing page, or call for a firm quote on your exact year, make, and model.

Car Key Replacement FAQ

Can you make a car key with no original?

Yes. If every key is lost, we can originate a new one from the vehicle's locks and immobilizer module. It takes slightly longer than a spare, so let us know when you book.

How long does it take?

Most spare keys are done in 20 to 45 minutes once we are with the car. All-keys-lost jobs and specialized European models can take 45 to 90 minutes.

Do I need to tow my car?

No. Our mobile vans are fully equipped workshops that come directly to your driveway, workplace, or roadside in Nashville.
